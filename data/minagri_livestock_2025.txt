DOCUMENT TITLE: MINAGRI Animal Resources & Livestock Production Report (2024/2025)
SOURCE: Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources (MINAGRI), Republic of Rwanda
SECTION: Animal Resources Directorate Annual Performance Report (2024/2025)

1. NATIONAL LIVESTOCK & ANIMAL PRODUCTION METRICS (2024/2025)
- Milk Production: 1,152,514 Metric Tons (MT)
- Meat Production: 219,523 Metric Tons (MT)
- Fish Production: 52,439 Metric Tons (MT)
- Eggs Production: 29,855 Metric Tons (MT)
- Honey Production: 8,460 Metric Tons (MT)

2. MANDATE & SPECIAL GOVERNMENT LIVESTOCK PROGRAMS
- Policy & Strategy Monitoring: Oversight and implementation of the Livestock Master Plan, Dairy Strategy, Meat Strategy, and Fish Master Plan.
- Nutrition & Malnutrition Initiatives: Implementation of the "Milk Drinking Campaign" and the "One Cup of Milk per Child" program in schools.
- Genetic & Animal Production Improvement: Genetic improvement programs targeted at increasing total livestock yields.
- Small Stock & Poultry Promotion: Promotion programs for small stock (sheep, goats, pigs, rabbits), poultry farming, and beekeeping.
