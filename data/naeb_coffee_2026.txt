DOCUMENT TITLE: NAEB Rwanda Coffee Sub-Sector Overview & Production Calendar
SOURCE: National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB), Republic of Rwanda
SECTION: Coffee Sub-Sector Background, Calendar, and Investment Opportunities

1. COFFEE SUB-SECTOR BACKGROUND & PRODUCTION METRICS
- Economic Impact: Major role in foreign exchange earnings and monetization of the rural economy.
- Farming Population: Approximately 400,000 smallholder farm families produce and depend on coffee.
- Cultivar & Cultivation Area: Introduced by Germans in early 1900s; dominated by 'Bourbon' type Arabica coffee across 42,000 hectares grown at altitudes under 1,900 meters.
- Annual Production Output: Ranges between 267,000 to 420,000 bags per year (16,000 MT to 21,000 MT).

2. COFFEE GROWING CALENDAR & HARVEST SEASON
- Crop Cycle: One crop per year.
- Flowering: Begins after the June–August dry season with the first rains in September.
- Harvesting Period: Begins late February in Western Province (shores of Lake Kivu), early March elsewhere, and extends through August in parts of Northern Province.

3. NAEB MANDATE & INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES
- NAEB Responsibilities: Technical assistance, planting materials, coffee processing support, global marketing, policy development, and sector strategy execution.
- Investment Areas: Coffee production, construction of washing stations, roasted coffee production, and setting up roasting plants/sales units in consuming countries.
