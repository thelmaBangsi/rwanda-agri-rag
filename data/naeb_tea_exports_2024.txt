DOCUMENT TITLE: NAEB Rwanda Tea Sector Performance & Projects Report (2023/2024)
SOURCE: National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB), Republic of Rwanda
SECTION: Tea Export Performance & Ongoing Development Projects

1. TEA SECTOR BACKGROUND & INFRASTRUCTURE
- Cultivated Area: Over 33,000 hectares planted with tea across Northern, Western, and Southern provinces.
- Farmer Organization: Over 50,000 tea farmers organized into 23 tea cooperatives and out-grower service companies.
- Processing Facilities: 19 privately owned operational tea factories.
- Key Associations: Tea cooperatives are grouped into 5 Unions under FERWACOTHE. Factories, cooperatives, packers, and federations operate under the Rwanda Tea Association (RTA).
- Tea Types Produced: Predominantly Black CTC tea. Also produces Green tea, White tea, Orthodox tea, and Organic tea.

2. PRODUCTION & EXPORT PERFORMANCE (2023/2024)
- Total Made Tea Production: 40,003 tons of made tea by June 2024 (up from 5,910 tons in 1980).
- Green Leaf Supply Distribution: 65% supplied by tea smallholders (farmers); 35% supplied by Industrial Blocs (IB).
- Export Revenue & Volume: Generated USD 114.8 million from export sales of 38,467.7 tons of made tea in the 2023/2024 fiscal year.
- Export Breakdown: 97.3% exported in raw form (80% sold via Mombasa Auctions, 17.3% sold directly, 2.7% consumed locally).

3. ONGOING TEA EXPANSION PROJECTS
- Rugabano Tea Project (Karongi District):
  * Target: 4,000 hectares for smallholders and 438 hectares for Industrial Bloc.
  * Status: 1,859 hectares planted (1,498 ha smallholders via Rugabano Service Company; 361 ha Industrial Bloc via Rugabano Tea Factory, operational since 2019).
- Nyaruguru Tea Project (Nyaruguru District - Kibeho, Munini, Busanze sectors):
  * Target: 6,000 hectares in partnership with Unilever / Lipton Tea.
  * Status: 2,843 hectares planted (2,108 ha smallholders via SCON South & North; 735 ha Kibeho Tea Factory Industrial Bloc). Lipton Tea Factory became operational in March 2024.
