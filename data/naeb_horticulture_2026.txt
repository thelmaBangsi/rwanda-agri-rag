DOCUMENT TITLE: NAEB Rwanda Horticulture Sub-Sector Overview & Incentives Report
SOURCE: National Agricultural Export Development Board (NAEB), Republic of Rwanda
SECTION: Horticulture Exports, Government Incentives, and GDP Contribution

1. HORTICULTURE SUB-SECTOR OVERVIEW
- Geographic Distribution: Production is distributed across all 30 districts of Rwanda.
- Product Categories: Vegetables, fruits, nuts, and flowers.
- Processed Exports: Pineapple juice, dried pineapple, mango juice, passion fruit juice, dried/canned vegetables, tomato paste, and strawberry jams.
- Export Destinations: Regional markets (Burundi, Democratic Republic of Congo, South Sudan) and global markets (EU, US).
- Economic Impact: Agriculture contributes 28% to Rwanda's total GDP.

2. GOVERNMENT INCENTIVES & COMPETITIVE FACTORS
- Land Access: Government facilitates access to private land and earmarks land for flower cultivation (including expropriation of private activities).
- Irrigation Subsidies: 50% subsidy on the importation of small-scale irrigation equipment. Identified irrigated schemes with developed facilities include Gabiro and ETI.
- Tax Incentives & Holidays: Tax exemptions on specified agricultural machinery and equipment. Up to a 7-year tax holiday depending on investment capital and business nature. Preferential corporate income tax rates ranging from 0% to 15%.
- Cold Chain & Logistics: Government offers cost-effective cold chain facilities and partners with the Private Sector Federation (PSF) to negotiate subsidized air-freight rates for exporters.
